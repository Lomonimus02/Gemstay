Staying

What does a GemStay include?+

A prepared home, with check-in and housekeeping as part of the stay. Airport pickup, a car, days out and dining discounts can be added when you book. Relocation and business-setup help are available for guests who need them.

How do I book?+

Open the home on GemStay, check the dates and the price, and book there. The team can also help on WhatsApp or by phone if you would rather talk it through.

Which offers are available?+

Selected stays include two extra days on a five-day booking, a yacht hour and a clean on seven nights, or a complimentary month on a three-month booking. Residents can combine the stay offers and receive 20% at selected restaurants. The team confirms what applies to the home and the dates.

Owning

What does GemStay manage?+

Listing preparation, guest bookings and communication, check-ins, housekeeping, maintenance, and monthly reporting. A property review confirms the services and terms for your home. There is no upfront listing fee — GemStay earns when the home does.

How accurate is the revenue calculator?+

The calculator is an illustrative planning tool. It uses sample nightly rates and occupancy assumptions, not live market data. It shows booking revenue, the costs you enter, and the income remaining before financing, taxes and acquisition costs. Ask for a property review for an estimate based on your home.

Does my property need to be furnished?+

Holiday homes need to be ready for guests, including DTCM standards. We inspect the property and can furnish it where it falls short. Scope and cost are agreed before any work begins.

Can I still use my property?+

Yes. Owner stays can be reserved in advance, subject to your agreement and any guest booking already in place. Contract length is discussed individually. Permits and insurance are issued for a year and can be renewed.

What if a guest damages the home?+

GemStay collects a security deposit and provides damage protection, so a guest stay does not rest on goodwill alone. The exact cover is part of the agreement.

How the calculation works

Available nights = 365 − owner stays. Booked nights = available nights × occupancy. Revenue = booked nights × nightly rate.

Management and platform fees each apply to gross revenue. Cleaning = booked nights ÷ average booking length × owner-paid cleaning cost. Monthly fixed costs are charged for all 12 months, including owner stays.

Income = revenue − all entered costs. Yield = annual income ÷ property value. Break-even is the occupancy needed to cover entered costs; “Not achievable” means this is impossible with the available nights and current inputs.
